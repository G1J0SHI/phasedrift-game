**PhaseDrift** is a dual-reality precision platformer where you shift between **Solar** and **Void** dimensions at any moment—flipping gravity while preserving every ounce of kinetic momentum.

Chain terminal velocity drops into mid-air phase shifts, pass through phase-locked terrain, and master high-speed wall jumps and ceiling launches across 50 campaign chambers.

---

### ✨ CORE FEATURES

- **🌌 Dual-Reality Physics** — Solar anchors gravity downward with solid Amber structures; Void inverts gravity upward with solid Cyan structures. Shift instantly at any moment.
- **⚡ Conserved Momentum** — Time a mid-air shift to slingshot horizontal and vertical velocity across spike pits, laser gates, and vertical shafts.
- **🏛️ 50 Sector Chambers** — Progress across 5 handcrafted sectors from _Solar Dawn_ to _Grand Singularity_ with crisp coyote time and jump buffering.
- **🎲 Quantum Drift Mode** — Play endless procedural anomalies or enter integer seeds to race identical randomized level layouts.
- **⏱️ Chrono-Shards & Speed Ranks** — Collect all 3 shards and clear under par time for **S Rank**, then race your holographic **PB Ghost**.
- **🎵 Adaptive Dual-Stem Audio** — Interactive soundtrack crossfades seamlessly between Solar and Void stems every time you shift realities.
- **🎮 Fully Customizable Ergonomics** — Tailor your run with customizable touch opacity/scale, Southpaw layout, gamepad support (Xbox/PlayStation), keyboard bindings, and haptic feedback.
- **🔒 100% Offline & Private** — No ads, no telemetry, no tracking, and no required accounts. All records and ghosts stay strictly on your device.
